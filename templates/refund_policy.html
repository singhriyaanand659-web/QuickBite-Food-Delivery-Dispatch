Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: August 2026 • QuickBite Technologies Inc.

At QuickBite, customer trust and satisfaction are our highest priorities. This policy outlines our cancellation windows, refund eligibility criteria, and payment reversal timelines.

💡 Quick Summary: 100% Free Cancellation on all orders prior to rider pickup. Automated instant refund initiation for prepaid UPI and Card payments.

1. Cancellation Windows & Rules

Order Stage Cancellation Status Refund Eligibility
Order Placed / Confirmed ✓ Allowed 100% Full Refund (Prepaid) / No Charge (COD)
Preparing / Rider Assigned ✓ Allowed 100% Full Refund (Prepaid) / No Charge (COD)
Picked Up / On The Way ✕ Restricted Contact Support Desk for special circumstances
Delivered ✕ Closed Report quality/missing item issues via Help Center

2. Refund Turnaround by Payment Method

Payment Method Turnaround Time Credit Destination
UPI (GPay / PhonePe / Paytm / BHIM) Instant (Demo) / 2–24 Hours (Prod) Original Linked Bank Account
Credit / Debit Cards Instant (Demo) / 2–4 Business Days (Prod) Original Card Account
Cash on Delivery (COD) Not Applicable Zero payment was collected; no refund required

3. How to Track Your Refund

Whenever a prepaid order is cancelled, a unique Refund ID (e.g. REF-84291) is automatically generated. You can track the 3-step refund status at any time:

  1. Refund Initiated: Cancellation accepted and refund recorded in backend registry.
  2. Processing: Bank gateway clearance and authorization verify the reversal.
  3. Completed: Money credited back to your original payment source.

Visit the Refund Status Tracker or click "View Refund Status" on any cancelled order in My Orders.

4. Need Assistance?

For urgent questions regarding a delayed refund, contact our 24/7 billing support team at refunds@quickbite.dispatch or message our support team on WhatsApp.
