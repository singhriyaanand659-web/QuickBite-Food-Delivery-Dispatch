1. Platform Service Overview

QuickBite provides an intelligent food ordering and dispatch platform connecting customers, partner restaurants, and delivery riders using Dijkstra shortest-path calculations and Min-Heap priority queue algorithms.

2. User Accounts & Ordering

  • Users agree to provide accurate delivery names, contact numbers, and valid graph delivery locations.
  • Orders placed are immediately queued in restaurant FIFO queues and assigned to the closest available rider.

3. Payment & Pricing

  • Prices displayed for menu items are determined by partner restaurants.
  • A nominal delivery charge of ₹5 and platform fee of ₹2 applies to each dispatched web order.
  • Supported payment options include Cash on Delivery, UPI, and Credit/Debit Cards with simulated sandbox processing.

4. Delivery Time & Dispatch Estimation

All Estimated Times of Arrival (ETA) are calculated dynamically based on weighted graph edge weights representing inter-node transit times. External real-world factors such as weather and kitchen rush may result in minor variations.
