Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026 • QuickBite Technologies Inc.

At QuickBite Food Delivery Dispatch, we are committed to safeguarding your personal data and privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, processed, and utilized in our food delivery dispatch ecosystem.

1. Information We Collect

To enable food ordering and optimize dispatch paths, we collect:

  • Customer Details: Name, contact phone number, and delivery street address.
  • Location Coordinates & Graph Nodes: Delivery location node names (e.g. Bandra, Dadar, Andheri) required for Dijkstra shortest path calculation.
  • Order & Cart Details: Selected restaurant ID, menu items, quantities, and order notes.
  • Payment Metadata: Transaction reference IDs, chosen payment method (COD, UPI, Card), and masked card/UPI identifiers. Real payment card data is never retained in plain text.

2. How We Use Your Information

Collected data is strictly utilized for the operational lifecycle of your order:

  • Routing and dispatching delivery riders using Min-Heap priority matching.
  • Calculating estimated time of arrival (ETA) using graph path weights.
  • Processing refunds to the originating source in the event of cancellation.
  • Facilitating official WhatsApp communication with support and delivery riders.

3. Data Protection & Security

We implement standard cryptographic safeguards and strict access controls. Simulated transactions and order registries are stored in secure memory environments.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell or monetize customer data. Relevant order details (Customer Name, Delivery Node, Item Summary) are shared exclusively with the assigned restaurant and dispatch rider for delivery fulfillment.

5. Contact Us Regarding Privacy

If you have any questions about this policy or your data rights, please contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@quickbite.dispatch.
